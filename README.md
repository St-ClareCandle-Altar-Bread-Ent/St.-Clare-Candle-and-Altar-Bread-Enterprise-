# 🕯️ St. Clare Candle and Altar Bread Enterprise

**Light for the Altar. Bread for the Soul.**

Handcrafted candles and consecration-ready altar bread from the Nuns of St. Clare in Ijebu-Ode, Ogun State, Nigeria.

---

## About

St. Clare Candle and Altar Bread Enterprise is a small-scale apostolate of the Poor Clare nuns of St. Clare Monastery Productions. We produce:

- **Altar Candles** — long-burning, dripless, suitable for liturgical use
- **Votive & Devotional Candles** — for private prayer and home shrines
- **Household Candles** — durable, clean-burning, every-day use
- **Emergency Candles** — long-burn, for power outages
- **Altar Bread** — pure wheat flour and water, consecration-ready

Every purchase supports the monastery's charitable works and creates dignified employment for women and youth in the Ijebu-Ode area.
