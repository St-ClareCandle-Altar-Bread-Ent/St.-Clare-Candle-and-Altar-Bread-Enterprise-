The Day Has Come and Gone

Her Vows, Her Yes, Her Joy

On Saturday, 24 October 2026, at 10:00 AM, Sister Maria Hope Ehizojie made her Simple Profession at St. Clare Monastery Nigeria, Ijebu-Ode Diocese — offering her life to God in the spirit of St. Clare of Assisi through the vows of poverty, chastity, and obedience.

The chapel was filled with joy. The Sisters, family, and friends gathered to witness her yes — and to give thanks to God for the gift of her vocation. Below are the photographs of a day the monastery will never forget.

Reflection

What This Day Means

The Simple Profession is the first public consecration of a Poor Clare Sister's life. It is a day of vows — poverty, chastity, and obedience — offered to God in the presence of the Church. It is not an end, but a beginning. The Simple Profession is followed, in time, by the Solemn Profession, in which the vows are confirmed forever.

Sister Maria Hope's yes is the seed of a lifelong yes. She has given herself to God in a new way — and the monastery, the diocese, and the whole Church have received her gift with gratitude.

Let us continue to pray for her. May the Lord who began this good work in her bring it to completion. May she persevere in joy. May her vows bear fruit for the Church and for the salvation of souls.

Coming in 2027

The Solemn Profession of Sister Agatha

Saturday, 27 February 2027 · 10:00 AM · St. Clare Monastery Nigeria, Ijebu-Ode

Next year, God willing, the monastery will celebrate another moment of grace: the Solemn Profession of Sister Agatha, on Saturday, 27 February 2027, at 10:00 AM. Unlike the Simple Profession, the Solemn Profession is a perpetual and final consecration. The Sister's vows are made forever, and she is bound to the monastery and to God in a total and irrevocable way.

We will share more details as the date approaches. For now, please keep Sister Agatha in your prayers as she prepares for this sacred and lifelong consecration — and continue to pray for Sister Maria Hope as she begins her life as a professed Poor Clare.
