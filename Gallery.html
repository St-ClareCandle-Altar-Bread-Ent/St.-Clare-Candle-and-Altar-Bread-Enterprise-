✦ The Season of Waiting ✦

Advent — A Time of Holy Expectation

Advent is the season in which the Church prepares her heart for the coming of Christ — His first coming in Bethlehem, and His final coming in glory. It is a season of waiting, but not passive waiting. It is the waiting of a bride for her bridegroom, of a people for their king, of a soul for its God.

✦

The Purple of Advent — In the Church's tradition, Advent is dressed in purple — the colour of penance, kingship, and expectation. It is the same purple worn during Lent, and it tells us that this is a season of preparation, not celebration; of longing, not fulfilment. But it is also the purple of royalty — for the King is coming, and His people are preparing His way.

At the centre of every parish's Advent is the Advent Wreath and its candles — three purple, one rose, and the white Christ Candle at the centre. Each Sunday of Advent, another candle is lit, and the Church draws closer to the mystery of the Incarnation. Together, the candles tell the story of our salvation — the long wait of Israel, the cry of the prophets, the joy of the Baptist, and at last the birth of the Light of the World.

St. Clare Candle and Altar Bread Enterprise crafts each Advent candle with the same care the Sisters give to the sanctuary candles — pure wax, clean wicks, steady flame, and a life that burns for the glory of God. Whether your parish needs a single Advent set or candles for every wreath in the church, we prepare each one in silence, prayer, and precision.

✦ The Light of the Risen Christ ✦

The Paschal Candle

At the heart of the Easter Vigil, in the darkness of the night, a single flame is struck from the new fire and carried into the church on the Paschal Candle — the Lumen Christi, the Light of Christ. It is the oldest and most sacred candle in the Church's liturgy, a living sign of the Resurrection, and the light that will burn in the sanctuary until Pentecost.

✝

The Sign of the Paschal Candle — The candle is marked with a cross, the Alpha and the Omega, and the year — a sign that Christ is the beginning and the end, the Lord of all time. Five grains of incense are fixed into the cross in memory of His wounds. And on Easter night, in the silent church, the Exsultet is sung, and the whole Church is drawn into the mystery of the Resurrection by the light of this one candle.

St. Clare Candle and Altar Bread Enterprise crafts the Paschal Candle with the reverence it deserves — pure, clean-burning wax; careful preparation of the wick; and a finish worthy of the sanctuary. Whether your parish needs a single large Paschal Candle for the Easter Vigil, or a smaller candle for the baptismal font, we prepare each one with the same prayer and precision that mark every work of our monastery.

The Paschal Candle is more than a symbol. It is a reminder that the light of Christ has overcome the darkness — and that the light we carry, in our parish, in our homes, in our lives, flows from that one Easter flame.
