📅 Saturday, 27 February 2027

Solemn Profession of Sister Agatha

The monastery joyfully announces the Solemn Profession of Sister Agatha, which will take place on Saturday, 27 February 2027, at 10:00 AM, at St. Clare Monastery Nigeria, Ijebu-Ode.

The Solemn Profession is a perpetual and final consecration — the Sister's vows made forever. Please remember Sister Agatha in your prayers as she prepares.
