How to Place an Order

Simple Steps to Order

1️⃣

Reach Out

Send us a WhatsApp message, call us, or email us. Tell us what you need and where you are.

2️⃣

Confirm Your Order

We will confirm quantities, pricing, and delivery. We may ask about liturgical requirements — host size, wax type, or feast day deadlines.

3️⃣

We Prepare It

Your candles are poured, cured, and boxed. Your altar bread is baked, inspected, sealed, and set aside for shipping.

4️⃣

Delivery or Pickup

We ship throughout Nigeria. Local customers are welcome to collect from the monastery by arrangement.

Common Questions

Quick Answers

How quickly will I hear back?

WhatsApp messages are usually answered within a few hours during business hours. Emails typically within 24 hours.

Can I pay online?

Payment is arranged directly with the Sisters — usually by bank transfer — once your order has been confirmed.

Do you deliver outside Ijebu-Ode?

Yes, we ship throughout Nigeria. Delivery arrangements depend on your location and the size of your order.

Can I visit the monastery in person?

Yes, but please contact us in advance. We are an enclosed community, but visitors are welcome for Mass or to collect orders.

Do you take custom orders?

Yes. Many of our products can be customized. Please let us know your requirements.

Can I order in bulk for my parish?

Absolutely. Use the Request a Bulk Quote button above and we will respond with a structured quote.
